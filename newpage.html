The Game of Cricket

Cricket is a bat and ball sport played between two teams. Each team has eleven players. The main goal is to chase the target given by the other team or make a score where other team has to defend.

Cricket Formats

🏏 Test Cricket

Test cricket is the longest format of cricket. A match can last up to five days and tests the skills and endurance of both teams.

🌏 ODI Cricket

One Day International cricket is a limited-overs format. Each team usually gets 50 overs.

⚡ T20 Cricket

T20 cricket is a fast-paced format where each team usually gets 20 overs.

Basic Rules

1. Scoring Runs

Batters score runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets.

2. Bowling

The bowler tries to get the batter out and stop them from scoring runs.

3. Fielding

Fielders try to stop the ball and prevent the batting team from scoring runs.
