Cricket Players

Cricket has many talented players from different countries. Here are some examples of famous cricket players and their roles in the game.

🏏 Batter

Virat Kohli (Indian)

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer known for his batting. He has represented India in different formats of cricket.

⚡ Fast Bowler

Jasprit Bumrah (Indian)

Jasprit Bumrah is an Indian fast bowler known for his accuracy, pace and different bowling variations.

Youngest male Indian cricketer to make an international debut

Vaibhav Suryavanshi

Vaibhav Suryavanshi is a young Indian cricket player. He became the youngest male Indian cricketer to make an international debut when he played his first T20 International match for India at 15 years old.

🌟 All-Rounder

Ravindra Jadeja

Ravindra Jadeja is an all-rounder who contributes with batting, bowling and fielding.

🏏 Batter

Kane Williamson

Kane Williamson is a New Zealand batter known for his calm approach and strong batting technique.
